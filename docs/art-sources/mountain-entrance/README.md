# Goblin Mountains entrance

Generated by `python3 tools/sprites/mountain.py` with the established pixelkit
pipeline. Runtime asset: `assets/images/mountain-entrance.bmp` (168×216 RGBA).
Seven tiles wide, nine high, with the south-facing cave door centered on the
approach entrance tile. No snow: the northern cold regions remain distinct.

Red sandstone canyon country, after classic fantasy mountain land art: a
stepped central massif with twin summits, a tall needle leaning in front of it
over the western road, a squat butte to the east and hazy distant mesas
behind. Creases split each mass into vertical slab planes that run from
sunlit orange on the west to warm violet shadow on the east. Edges break into
small ledges and teeth, broken strata cross the faces with sunlit lips, and
ledge tops glow gold. Fallen blocks gather at the base. Rough-cut stone jambs,
goblin timber, iron spikes, dark war banners and the goblin face sigil frame
the cave mouth.

The collision footprint tapers with the silhouette: three tiles wide on the
top two rows, five on the next two and seven below. Save version 218 moves
players and loot standing on the newly raised rows onto the road.

`scale-comparison.png` shows the mountain beside the crypt at the same native
scale (enlarged 3× with nearest-neighbor sampling). `renderer-preview.png` uses
the actual SDL game renderer with town grass, cobblestones and the road.
The mountain landmark occupies the same coordinates when approached from either
town; both directions start the interior climb at level 1.
