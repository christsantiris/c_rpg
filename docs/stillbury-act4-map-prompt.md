# Stillbury Act 4 atlas edit

Built-in imagegen edits of the previous canonical `docs/world-map.png`.
The selected output replaces that same permanent file; README keeps that path
and uses the first 12 characters of the replacement's SHA-256 checksum.

## Edit specification

Use case: precise-object-edit. Preserve the 1536×1024 illustrated atlas, gold
border, title, compass, typography, town/landmark positions, unrelated roads,
river and geography south of it. Preserve the snowy Frostfell river source,
snow-free Giant's Peaks, Giant's Bridge, Stillbury Docks, Saltwind skiff crossing
and island voyage.

Make the sole first castle approach Stillbury north through Blackwater Swamp,
Royal Catacombs and the explicitly labeled Crown Road North. Add a continuous
limestone escarpment north of the quarry/Labyrinth, ending their local road
without passage to Rosemoor or the castle. Add a northern ridge separating
Elderwood and Sunken Coast from Ridgeshire, retaining the eastern coastal road.
Keep the central northern corridor open.

Keep Castle Grounds' lateral Crown Roads to Rosemoor and Ridgeshire. Remove
the castle-to-Ashen road and draw Ashen's access from Rosemoor around the
western foothills, without a junction into the castle. Retain Rosemoor's
Frostfell access, Ridgeshire's northern Dragonspine road and eastern Ironfang
road; remove the obsolete southern Ridgeshire road stub.

Move the Escape Tunnel route toward Stillbury with a south-pointing dashed
underground arrow labeled ESCAPE ONLY. No connection to Ridgeshire. Add a
discreet ACT V — PROVISIONAL legend over the northern later-act regions.
Do not invent places, crossings or additional connections.

## Targeted corrections

The second edit removes Ridgeshire's southern stub and makes its short northern
Dragonspine road explicit. Subsequent localized edits restore the yellow dashed
Stillbury–Saltwind skiff crossing and white dashed Stillbury Docks island voyage
after the correction omitted them. The final correction requests only that
white maritime line while preserving all geography and the yellow crossing.

The western resurrection shrine is follow-up work and is not shown yet.
