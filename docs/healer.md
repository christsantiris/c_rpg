# Stillbury healer

The **HEALER** building stands north of Stillbury's east-west road. Walk inside,
approach **Lysa**, and press **T** (or your remapped Talk key), as you would talk
to an NPC in the Inn.

Lysa shows your current HP and gold, plus the exact HP gain and price for each
choice:

- **1 — Full:** restore all missing HP.
- **2 — Half:** restore half of your missing HP, rounded up.
- **3 — Affordable:** restore as much HP as your available gold can buy, up to full health.

Treatment costs **1 gold per 10 HP**, rounded up. An asterisk marks an option
that costs more gold than you have. Healing is immediate and uses no inventory
space. It restores HP only. At full health, Lysa charges nothing and explains
that no treatment is needed.

Press **Esc** or **Enter** to close the dialogue. Walk out through the south door
to return to the path in front of the healer. Saving inside preserves your
location, HP, gold, and any open treatment offer.

Lysa no longer buys or sells Health Potions. The Alchemist and Rosemoor's
Apothecary still sell them for 20 gold each.

## Stillbury witch

The **WITCH** hut stands east of Stillbury's crossroads. Walk inside, approach
**Morwen**, and press **T** (or your remapped Talk key). She offers the same
three choices for **MP**: full restoration, half of your missing MP, or as much
as your gold can afford. The price is **1 gold per 10 MP**, rounded up.

Use **1**, **2**, or **3** to choose the displayed amount and price. **Esc** or
**Enter** closes the dialogue. Walk through the south door to return to the path
in front of her hut. Saving inside preserves your location and any open offer.

Morwen restores MP only and no longer buys or sells Mana Potions. The Alchemist
and Rosemoor's Apothecary still sell them for 20 gold each.
