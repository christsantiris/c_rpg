# Sunken Coast and Flooded Ruins

The Sunken Coast is an eight-stage adventure entered through the south town
gate. It has an independent level cache and progression record, so visiting
another region does not reset explored coast stages.

## Progression

Stages alternate among north, south, east, and west entrances and exits. A
stage never uses the same edge for both, preventing the player from immediately
walking back into the stage they just completed. Stages 1–7 can be exited
without clearing every enemy. The stage 8 exit remains sealed until the
Drowned Queen is defeated, then returns the player to the south town road.

The region moves from storm-lashed beaches into increasingly submerged ruins:

1. Shattered Strand
2. Tidepool Causeway
3. Siren Cliffs
4. Drowned Courtyard
5. Temple of the Deep
6. Flooded Galleries
7. Serpent Channel
8. The Drowned Throne

## Enemy roster

- **Illusion:** a one-hit spectral decoy that still threatens careless players.
- **Merfolk:** the dependable spear-wielding frontline enemy.
- **Siren:** a fragile ranged attacker whose song strikes along clear lanes.
- **Giant Crab:** a heavily armored bruiser that advances every other turn.
- **Animated Statue:** the toughest regular defender; slow, durable, and hard
  to damage.
- **Water Elemental:** a mid-range attacker that sends water surges down clear
  lanes.
- **Sea Serpent:** a late-region predator with high attack and mobility.
- **Drowned Queen:** the stage 8 boss. She remains dormant in her throne
  clearing until the player enters, then uses telegraphed tidal waves.

## Visual identity

Coast floors use deep blue water, teal highlights, and occasional sand-colored
debris. Walls resemble algae-covered drowned masonry. Entrances and exits are
framed as glowing ruin arches, and the town gate repeats the same teal stone
language so its destination is clear before the player enters.
