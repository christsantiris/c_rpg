# Act 3 eastern arc — Dawnmere and Saltwind

Status: implemented first pass. The playable route and canonical README atlas
now use the geography below. The wider Act 4 story and western continuation
after Sunscar remain deferred.

## Established direction

- Act 1 covers arrival in Oakhaven and the Lich King. Act 2 offers the Haunted
  Forest and Goblin Mountains in either order, with either or both completable.
- The eastern Act 3 route follows the Haunted Forest to Dawnmere. Dawnmere stays
  on Oakhaven's bank of the main river, east of the forest.
- Dawnmere is a rebel town occupied by royal troops. Its townsfolk ask the
  player to liberate it. Royal soldiers from the Crown Roads supply the
  occupation roster, including formation combat.
- Saltwind follows Dawnmere and leads to a river/estuary crossing. The player
  uses the existing style of skiff navigation, docks at Stillbury, and enters
  the town to start Act 4.
- Stillbury moves to the opposite bank from Oakhaven, in the area currently
  occupied by Elderwood on the atlas. Either Act 3 route reaches this town.
- Publicly known anti-Crown actions affect Stillbury's treatment of the player.
  Liberation of Dawnmere is a public act. Checkpoints can demand surrender;
  royal troops become hostile if the player refuses or resists. Civilians have
  different loyalties and responses.
- The King remains the apparent source of the kingdom's troubles. The Royal
  Arcanist's involvement remains undisclosed.
- The western continuation after Sunscar remains open. Ironfang Mines joins
  Stonewake Quarry, Blackwater Swamp and Elderwood as a candidate.

## Proposed story sequence

| Beat | Player experience | Story consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| News in Oakhaven | Existing NPCs describe confiscations and royal occupation in Dawnmere. | The royal documents from Act 2 connect to an immediate human crisis. |
| First arrival | Royal banners, a checkpoint and shut businesses introduce occupied Dawnmere. The western retreat stays open. | The player sees how the Crown justifies its actions against alleged rebels. |
| Local contact | Watchkeeper Seren asks for help from a sheltered room in the Alehouse. | The player learns the occupation's targets and can accept the liberation quest. |
| Liberation | Defeat the garrison positions and occupying commander. Remaining garrison troops withdraw. | Dawnmere becomes a safe base; the player's intervention becomes public. |
| Onward discovery | The command post contains orders directing seized goods and detainees through Stillbury's customs house. | Stillbury becomes the next place to investigate. |
| Marsh passage | Seren explains that the normal crossing remains controlled by royal forces. A neglected route through Saltwind offers another way across. | The player has a reason to explore the marsh instead of immediately boarding a town ferry. |
| Final landing | Defeat Sszareth, who controls the old northern landing and its narrow access channel. | The player can reach the river passage without making Sszareth another royal ally. |
| River crossing | Board the skiff, paddle across navigable water, and dock outside Stillbury. | The route physically reaches the shared Act 4 destination. |
| Town entrance | Walk from the dock to Stillbury's entrance. Its authorities recognise the Dawnmere liberator as an enemy of the Crown. | Act 4 starts with a different reception from a character whose anti-Crown actions remain unknown. |

Saltwind now returns to Dawnmere at its entrance and opens the river crossing
after Sszareth at its final launch. See [current Saltwind gameplay](saltwind-marsh.md).

## Chapter 1: Dawnmere Under Occupation

### Opening in Oakhaven

Use the existing cast rather than adding a new quest giver to Oakhaven:

- Bram hears that merchants arriving from the east have lost their goods to
  royal soldiers. He offers the rumour through optional Inn conversation.
- Alder's post-Necromancer conversation points onward to Dawnmere and confirms
  that the forest road now provides access. It also acknowledges whether the
  player has found one or both royal documents.
- Aldric can comment that organised royal troops will fight differently from
  the creatures in the forest. His recruitment remains optional.

The rumour is available before the forest boss is defeated, but reaching
Dawnmere still requires opening the forest road. Hearing it neither accepts
a quest nor consumes one of the three active quest slots.

Suggested rumour: "Dawnmere refused the Crown's new levies. Royal soldiers have
taken its streets, and traders say the confiscations haven't stopped."

This supplies a concrete reason for the town's resistance without establishing
that every resident shares one political view.

### Arrival and contact

The player arrives through the western gate. A royal checkpoint controls the
main street; the initial arrival space and retreat to the forest remain usable.
The gate guard orders the player to turn back from the restricted street. Moving past
the clearly marked checkpoint or attacking the soldiers starts local combat.

A short side lane reaches the Alehouse, where Seren and displaced residents
have taken shelter. Before liberation this is a meeting place, not a normal
operating shop. Civilians do not wander into combat or become attack targets.

Seren is a local watchkeeper who resisted the occupation. Reuse her identity;
her existing Stormwatch adventure can be placed in a later act. Torren and
Corvin remain townspeople rather than additional main quest givers.

Suggested Seren explanation: "They call us rebels because we refused to give
them everything we own. Their commander holds the square and the waterfront.
Break his hold, and we can take our town back. Stillbury will hear of it."

Accepting the quest gives the journal a clear objective. The physical encounter
and its completion work even if the player never speaks to Seren.

### Liberation encounter

Use one town map with three substantial positions rather than five town stages:

1. **Western checkpoint:** a small Royal Guard and Road Archer squad introduces
   advancing under covering fire, with a side-lane approach.
2. **Market square:** the broadest fighting space supports a guard line and
   rear archers. Buildings provide alternate approaches and cover.
3. **Waterfront command post:** an existing Royal Guard becomes the named
   occupying commander, provisionally Captain Rusk, backed by a final squad. He uses the royal squad
   tactics; this proposal does not require a new enemy species or spell set.

The first position secures retreat and nearby lanes; the square and commander
complete liberation. The player can choose how to approach each position.
Completion does not require finding every surviving soldier. Remaining
garrison troops retreat and do not respawn on later town visits.

Royal Guards form the front line; Road Archers seek useful rear firing lanes.
Horsemen are optional at the exterior gate or a suitably wide courtyard.
Narrow streets break formations naturally. Soldiers receive ordinary movement
and attack opportunities, with no additional actions for being in formation.
Bandits and Blighted Wolves are not part of this occupation roster.

The migration from Crown Roads concerns royal troop encounters. The eventual
role and remaining encounters of those roads belong to their later redesign.

### Liberation payoff

- Royal banners and the occupied-state presentation change to a liberated town.
- Seren returns to the square; townsfolk and existing businesses resume their
  normal roles. The Belt Maker remains Dawnmere's specialist shop.
- Dawnmere supplies ordinary journey essentials after liberation. It does not
  duplicate Oakhaven's Goldsmith or Soulstone Shrine.
- Award a one-time XP reward and a modest purse of recovered garrison pay.
  Exact amounts need a balance pass; the player should not be paid twice for
  completing the encounter before accepting its quest.
- The command post yields a readable royal order naming Stillbury's customs
  house as the destination for confiscated cargo and detainees. It uses the
  Evidence journal rather than inventory capacity or an active quest slot.
- Seren warns that royal couriers will identify the player as Dawnmere's
  liberator. The consequence is explained before departure.

The evidence advances the investigation from royal sponsorship of creatures
to the Crown's treatment of people. It does not identify the Arcanist or prove
what ultimately happens to the detainees.

The liberation quest moves to **Completed** when the garrison is broken. An
optional conversation with Seren acknowledges the victory and offers the
follow-up, **Passage Through Saltwind**. It is not a required reward turn-in or
an additional gate on the onward route. If objectives are already fulfilled,
Seren acknowledges them rather than assigning the same task again.

## Chapter 2: Passage Through Saltwind

### Reason for the journey

Dawnmere's former public crossing is under royal control. Liberating the town
does not dislodge the forces holding the main river channel. Seren knows an
older route through Saltwind, where fishing landings link sheltered islands
and a final launch site reaches the navigable river below the rapids.

Saltwind is dangerous because local creatures have taken over its abandoned
landings. Sszareth controls the last one. He is not proposed as another agent
of the King; the occupation made this neglected route necessary.

Recommended gate: Dawnmere's liberation opens access to the marsh waterfront.
Accepting either main quest is optional. Sszareth's defeat opens the final
crossing route, while retreat through earlier stages remains available.

### Five-stage progression

| Stage | Main route | Optional exploration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 — Saltwind Landing | Reach the old fishing landing and learn the existing board, paddle and dock controls. | Nearby supplies provide a short preparation detour. |
| 2 — Splitroot Delta | Choose between channels connecting the onward shore. | Search Corvin's stranded spice skiff and read its manifest. |
| 3 — Mudback Pools | Navigate the broad pools and fight through the occupied landing. | Recover Corvin's cargo on the existing guarded side island. |
| 4 — Stiltwater Village | Use the abandoned village's decks and landings to approach the final channel. | Explore houses and side platforms for finite supplies or a chance at magic loot. |
| 5 — Heartroot Sanctuary | Defeat Sszareth and gain access to the northern launch. | Existing off-route supplies remain optional. |

Every stage retains a usable incoming landing and retreat route. Ordinary
enemies need not all die to progress. Stage 5's final launch is visibly blocked
by Sszareth's hold on the landing, rather than an unexplained invisible barrier.
The existing freely accessible final departure needs an explicit redesign to
support this story gate.

Start with one optional eastern side quest: **The Missing Spice Skiff**. It
reuses Corvin, the stage-2 manifest and stage-3 cargo. Frame the lost shipment
as an attempt to avoid the occupation's confiscations. Corvin pays on the next
return to Dawnmere; recovery never requires returning before crossing to
Stillbury. Findings count even before acceptance. The western branch can
later supply one or two additional side quests for the act.

Side caches and village exploration are discoveries, not extra automatically
accepted quests. The journal's three-active limit stays meaningful.

### Crossing and Act 4 boundary

The last launch connects to a short, playable river reach. Reuse the skiff's
existing interaction pattern:

1. Stand at the landing and press the configured Interact key to board.
2. Paddle through the marked navigable channel with the movement controls.
3. Reach Stillbury's eastern dock and use Interact to disembark.
4. Walk the dock approach and enter Stillbury. **This town entrance starts
   Act 4**, not defeating Sszareth or merely boarding the boat.

This is player-controlled travel, with no ferry menu, fare, cutscene arrival,
boat damage, naval combat or new boating resource. The crossing lies below
Oakhaven's rapids and does not make their broken ferry usable. The northern
dock approach leads along the bank to Stillbury; the boat never passes through
the rapids to reach it.

The party travels with the player when boarding and disembarks together. An
incapacitated companion stays incapacitated; boarding is not resurrection.
The boat, boarding state, current shore and return route survive save/load.
Leaving town must provide a usable return to the marsh and Dawnmere, including
when Stillbury's authorities are hostile.

The landing provides room to disembark and retreat. Stillbury's checkpoint
stands inside its town entrance, beyond the dock approach. The player is not
attacked while unable to leave the boat. Entering Stillbury establishes the
Act 4 arrival even if the authorities then demand surrender; being wanted
does not prevent that arrival trigger. The arrest outcome and the rest of Stillbury's quests belong
to the Act 4 design, not this eastern story proposal.

## Progression, rewards and consequences

- The Goblin King and Sunscar are not prerequisites for the eastern route.
- Complete the eastern encounters without requiring prior NPC conversations.
- Dawnmere's liberation and marsh passage are successive main quests, keeping
  one eastern main objective active at a time.
- The passage quest completes when the player reaches the Stillbury dock. The
  act changes when the player subsequently enters the town.
- Liberation is a public anti-Crown event. Stillbury's checkpoints demand
  surrender; refusal or resistance leads to hostile troops. Civilian responses
  vary, and some contacts can still help the player.
- The western route can later set the same public-recognition state through
  its own known anti-Crown acts. Possessing secret evidence alone is not an
  automatically reported crime.
- Returning to finish the other Act 3 route remains possible. Entering Act 4
  does not discard unfinished quests, remove regional rewards or close boats.
- Balance the route so one Act 3 branch can reach Act 4. Completing both still
  yields additional XP, gold and opportunities for magic equipment.

Recommended Return to Town behaviour: occupied Dawnmere does not replace the
last safe town. After liberation it becomes a valid safe destination. Wanted
Stillbury retains the last safe anchor. Submitting to its checkpoint turns the
player back toward the dock; resisting starts guard combat. Imprisonment and
the larger Stillbury storyline remain part of the later Act 4 design.

## First-pass implementation notes

- Captain Rusk is a named Royal Guard; three squads engage by proximity or attack.
  Guards form melee shoulders and archers seek rear firing lanes. Civilians are
  not combat targets. Retiring royal road patrols leaves bandits and wolves there.
- Defeating the checkpoint, market squad and Rusk awards 100 gold and 500 XP once,
  completes liberation without a required acceptance/turn-in, and saves his royal
  confiscation orders in Evidence. The surviving command squad withdraws.
- Seren shelters in the Alehouse while occupied and returns to the square after
  liberation. Its host then sells health/mana potions and arrows; Corvin's optional
  cargo recovery remains separate. The Belt Maker reopens.
- Both eastern main quests obey the three-active limit; finished objectives are
  Completed. The passage completes at Stillbury's landing; entering town sets
  the Act 4 arrival. Secret Act 2 parchments alone are not a public crime.
- The town entrance uses a short south-facing bank approach so northbound travel
  remains northbound through the town doorway. This is Stillbury's river landing,
  not the retired eastern mountain gate. Other new town exits are deferred.
- Stillbury's authorities challenge known anti-Crown actions. Submitting or
  withdrawing returns to the dock; resisting activates persistent guards. Civilian
  reactions differ; supplies and the boat retreat remain usable.
