# Dawnmere's eastern area — concept shortlist

The selected concept is now playable: [Stormwatch Headland](stormwatch-headland.md).

Status: the table below records the initial brainstorming. Stonewake is now
implemented in Glassdeep's former location south of Stillbury; see
[Stonewake Quarry](stonewake-quarry.md) and the [approved art direction](stonewake-quarry-proposal.md).
Stormwatch Headland occupies the coast east of Dawnmere. Ashen Hollow
occupies the western volcanic foothills beyond Rosemoor. Catacombs will retain its identity and receive a later gameplay
revamp. Blackwater, Sunken Coast and Frostfell remain in place.

The area belongs east of lowland, coastal Dawnmere. Goblin Mountains remains
north of town and Ridgeshire remains the elevated mountain city. Saltwind Marsh
retains its sea-level southern inlet. Use the open eastern headland without
moving existing regions, changing the Sunken Coast cove, altering Glassdeep's
beaches or breaking any town road or dock. The atlas shows the watchfort and its eastern road.

## How the candidates compare

**Stormwatch** gives the clearest regional identity at this location: a compact
fort landmark above coastal grassland, with exposed approaches and sheltered
rooms. It would keep travel and combat ashore. Wind warnings must be turn-based,
readable and safe around boundaries; a gust must never throw anyone off-map or
into unreachable terrain.

**Stonewake** is the strongest alternative for spatial exploration. Its quarry
cuts, ramp loops and crane crossings give the minimap a distinct outline and
provide useful optional routes. It must look like excavation in low coastal
rock, rather than another mountain range surrounding Dawnmere. All classes
operate the cranes through Interact.

**Amberhollow** offers the freshest enemy family and a warm, vivid palette.
Surface and burrow sections should create multiple approaches within each
stage, with visible entrances and a readable minimap. Scent lures redirect living
enemies without despawning them or making them invisible.

**The Shattered Observatory** offers a magical exploration system. Stable arch
destinations matter: it should reward learning the layout without moving
already explored rooms or changing the player's orientation unexpectedly. Its
main mechanic is spatial travel, keeping Glassdeep's light-and-crystal puzzles
distinct.

**Harrowfields** most strongly establishes Dawnmere's surrounding countryside
and supports a dark fairytale mood. Its crop-cutting concept overlaps Elderwood's
bramble routes more than the other options; it would need more refinement to
earn a distinct mechanical identity. Crop cover must never allow an invisible
enemy to attack the player.

## Shared design standards

Develop the selected theme as five forward stages, entered at stage 1 through
a landmark on the eastern approach. Give the stages different compositions and
landmarks rather than five variations of one corridor. Use connected loops,
optional rooms or clearings, identifiable terrain boundaries and reachable
side rewards. Entrance and exit tiles belong at terrain boundaries and should
match this area's art. The boss opens the final departure back to the approach.

Preserve the existing game rules: retreat and portals retain the expedition;
fresh physical entry respawns ordinary enemies; boss victories and one-time
rewards persist. Every class can use the exploration mechanic. Enemy positions
must remain reachable and visible, and character art must match the established
scale. A unique generator must validate the complete walking area, objectives,
optional rewards, spawns and exits across varied generated layouts.

The next discussion should choose the theme, then refine its five stage
identities, enemy power order, core interaction and boss. Quest objectives and
rewards can follow that decision. Corvin's planned Saltwind quest remains a
separate design task.
