# Watchfires of Ridgeshire

**Marshal Veyra** waits beside the planning table in **Ridgeshire's Town Hall**.
Approach her and press **T**, then **Y** to accept this one-time quest. The town's
warning fires have fallen dark, leaving its scouts unable to signal danger.

| Destination | Route from Ridgeshire | Added defenders |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Goblin Mountains, stage 6 | South mountain gate; enter stage 7 and continue toward stage 6 | Two Goblin Archers and two Hobgoblin Guards |
| Ashen Hollow, stage 3 | North gate | One Obsidian Guardian and two Cinder Imps |
| Dragonspine, stage 3 | East gate | One Giant, one Goblin Archer, and one Fire Elemental |

Each watchfire occupies an ordinary floor in its stage's middle clearing.
Complete the three objectives in any order. Defeat the assigned Watchfire
Defenders and any ordinary enemies within four tiles, then press **A** while
standing on or beside the fire to repair and light it. Luring a quest defender
away does not bypass the encounter. Ordinary enemies elsewhere do not block
repairs, and regional bosses are optional.

Repairs consume no inventory items or gold. Restored stone beacons display an
animated flame on subsequent visits. The fires mark quest progress without
changing ordinary enemy populations or difficulty.

Return to Veyra for **180 gold and 1,400 score**, awarded once. The journal
lists each objective's region, stage, and completion status and retains them
in the Completed tab after claiming the reward.

Accepting the quest preserves explored maps, existing enemies, boss victories,
and portals. Dedicated defenders are added when each stage is visited, including
previously cleared stages. Their damage and defeats persist through revisits
and saves; they do not replenish after their encounter has been placed.

Save version **122** stores quest state, restored-fire progress, and encounter
placement. Older saves receive an available quest without losing progress.
Saves inside the Town Hall gain Veyra, with overlapping players and dropped
items moved safely beside her.
