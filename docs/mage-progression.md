# Planned Mage Progression

The Mage should eventually use a magic-based primary attack instead of relying
on physical bump attacks. Mage weapons should support casting, chiefly by
increasing maximum mana or improving other magical attributes, rather than
competing with Warrior and Rogue weapons for physical damage.

The Alchemist should become the Mage's main progression vendor. Later work can
add new, stronger spells and a system for leveling up spells the player already
knows. This larger redesign is intentionally separate from the current weapon
and blacksmith progression work.

The current staff path provides temporary equipment progression:

| Tier | Weapon | Current role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Common | Staff | Starter weapon with +2 spell power |
| Uncommon | Runed Staff | Midgame weapon with +4 spell power |
| Rare | Magic Staff | Capstone weapon with +8 spell power |
