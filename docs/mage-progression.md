# Mage Progression

The Mage begins with 70 HP, 100 MP, 4 attack, 2 defense, a Staff, and a Scroll:
Magic Arrow. Use the scroll from the inventory, equip Magic Arrow in the
spellbook, and cast with `C`. The class is built around ranged spell damage
rather than sustained melee combat.

## Mana Recovery

- Gaining a level increases maximum HP by 10 and restores both HP and MP to
  maximum.
- Mana Potions restore 20 MP and are sold by the Alchemist.
- Morwen's witch hut also sells Mana Potions for 20 gold.
- Lysa sells Health Potions for 20 gold. The Alchemist sells both potion types.

Speak with Rook in the Tavern to open the labyrinth. Returning his stolen
ivory rook rewards 40 gold and 500 score once.

## Spell Progression

The Alchemist sells Magic Arrow, Fireball, and Heal scrolls from the start.
Regional boss victories unlock the Mage-only progression stock:

| Regional bosses defeated | New stock |
| ---: | --- |
| 1 | Tome: Magic Arrow II and Scroll: Frost Bolt |
| 2 | Tome: Fireball II and Scroll: Teleport |
| 3 | Tome: Heal II |

Upgrade tomes require the original spell to be known. Magic Arrow, Fireball,
and Heal can reach rank 3 by using another matching tome. Frost Bolt damages
the first target in its line and freezes a surviving target for two turns.
Teleport moves up to four open tiles in the last movement direction and spends
no MP when the path is completely blocked.

## Mage Equipment

Staves are two-handed weapons. Their spell power is added directly to spell
damage and healing.

| Tier | Weapon | Attack | Casting bonuses |
| ---: | --- | ---: | --- |
| 1 | Staff | +4 | +2 spell power, +10 maximum MP |
| 2 | Runed Staff | +6 | +4 spell power, +20 maximum MP, 5% cheaper spells |
| 3 | Magic Staff | +9 | +8 spell power, +35 maximum MP, 10% cheaper spells |

Mage robes trade physical defense for maximum MP and, at higher tiers, reduced
spell costs. Staff and robe cost reductions stack to a maximum of 50%. See
[Weapons, Armor, and Shields](weapons.md) for the full equipment catalog.
