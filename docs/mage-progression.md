# Mage Progression

The Mage should eventually use a magic-based primary attack instead of relying
on physical bump attacks. Mage weapons should support casting, chiefly by
increasing maximum mana or improving other magical attributes, rather than
competing with Warrior and Rogue weapons for physical damage.

The Alchemist is the Mage's primary progression vendor. Boss victories unlock
Magic Arrow II and Frost Bolt after one boss, Fireball II and Teleport after
two bosses, and Heal II after three bosses. Upgrade tomes require the Mage to
know the original spell.

Frost Bolt damages a target and freezes it for two turns. Teleport moves the
Mage up to four open tiles in the aimed direction and costs no mana when its
path is completely blocked.

The current staff path provides temporary equipment progression:

| Tier | Weapon | Current role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Common | Staff | +2 spell power, +10 maximum mana |
| Uncommon | Runed Staff | +4 spell power, +20 mana, 5% cheaper spells |
| Rare | Magic Staff | +8 spell power, +35 mana, 10% cheaper spells |

Staff and robe mana-cost reductions stack to a maximum of 50%.
