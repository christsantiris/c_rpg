# Act 3: the western route

The western arc has **three quests**: two main adventures and one optional
crown delivery. It ends on entering Stillbury, where Act 4 begins. There is no
paid royal-service branch and no magic-lamp quest.

| Quest | Assigned by | Objectives | Completion and reward |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The King's Tribute | Zara, first in Oakhaven's Inn, then at the caravan camp outside Sunscar | Reclaim the confiscated royal convoy cargo in Sunscar 2 **and** recover the Pharaoh's agreement by defeating him in Sunscar 5 | Objectives move to Completed immediately. Return to Zara at the camp immediately outside Sunscar for **200 gold and 500 XP**, once. |
| Break the Jarl's Blockade | Caravan Scout Garran at the camp | Defeat the Last Jarl in Giant's Peaks 5 | Completes on the boss's defeat, without returning to Garran. The Jarl's existing **200 gold and 2,200 score** reward remains unchanged. |
| Giant's Crown | Historian Liora at the camp | Recover the crown directly from the defeated Jarl | Deliver it to Antiquarian Edda beside the Stillbury end of Giant's Bridge, **outside the checkpoint**, for **220 gold, 1,600 score and a Stoneguard Shield**. Edda reserves the shield if the pack is full. |

After the Goblin King's defeat, Dain directs the player to Zara in Oakhaven's
Inn. On the first visit to the Sunscar road, Zara moves to the caravan camp.
Players who skip that Oakhaven conversation can still meet her at the camp and
accept her quest there. She offers only The King's Tribute. Garran and Liora
have separate conversations and explicit `Y` acceptance / `N` decline.
The usual three-active-quest limit applies. Finished objectives free a slot
before any reward hand-in.

## The King's Tribute and the Crown

The convoy has seven named royal escorts, including archers. It occupies a
side clearing in Sunscar 2, leaving the onward and retreat routes usable. The
escorts hold their posts until provoked. Aldric will not attack them first.
Inspect the cargo with Interact (`A` by default): `Y` challenges the convoy;
`N`, Enter or Escape lets the player move along. Direct attacks also provoke
the escort and count as a public anti-Crown action. Once the escort is defeated,
Interact at the cargo to recover it. Recovery uses no inventory capacity.

Attacking the convoy is reported to Stillbury, just as liberating Dawnmere is.
It gives the existing colder checkpoint reception. Passing the convoy leaves
that standing unchanged. Defeating the Pharaoh or Jarl alone is not a public
anti-Crown offense. Prior reported actions on either route still apply.

The Pharaoh's agreement bears the King's seal: confiscated goods go to the
Pharaoh as tribute, accounted for through Stillbury customs. The Jarl's royal
charter authorizes his blockade and permits royal shipments through it. Both
are retained in the journal's Evidence tab, deepening suspicion of the King
without revealing the Royal Arcanist.

## Continuing the journey

Sunscar's final exit returns to the roadside camp for Zara's reward. Continue
past its pyramid entrance and follow the desert road north to Giant's Peaks.
The existing five-stage journey and Jarl gate work from either entrance;
retreat stays available. His death yields the crown directly, without an
inscription, seal, or quest vault. The retired vault remains ordinary optional
treasure with breakable masonry.

No NPC dialogue or quest acceptance is required to enter either region, fight
its boss or cross the opened pass. Cargo, agreement and crown discoveries count
if found before accepting their quests. Edda's position allows a wanted player
to complete the delivery before attempting Stillbury's checkpoint.

Save version **240** persists all three quests, Zara's move and Crown standing.
Existing maps, wounds, escort casualties, ordinary loot and boss victories are
retained. Retired lamp quest progress and decorations are removed without a
reward. Earlier crown discoveries and rewards remain recorded; a prior Jarl
victory now supplies the crown directly.
