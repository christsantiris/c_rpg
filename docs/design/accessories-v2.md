# Accessory expansion

All accessories use the two existing accessory slots. Two rings, including two
copies of the same ring, are allowed. Every other category has a limit of one.
All classes may equip every accessory. Maximum-resource bonuses preserve current
HP and MP on equip and clamp them on removal; equipping never grants healing.

Tier 1 is Common and available immediately. Tier 2 is Uncommon and unlocks after
one regional boss victory. Tier 3 is Rare and unlocks after two. Repeat Labyrinth
victories do not advance stock. Earlier tiers remain available. Buy prices are
twice base value; selling returns base value divided by four, rounded down.

The Cobbler stands southeast of Stillbury's square. The Belt Maker stands
southwest of Dawnmere's square. Enter the building and Talk beside its resident
to buy or sell. Each specialist only buys its own categories; boots and belts
are excluded from Blacksmith sales. The Leather Cap remains at the Blacksmith.

Save version 228 appends both new location IDs and accessory category IDs without
renumbering earlier IDs. Existing Stillbury and Dawnmere maps gain their shops
in place; players and stacked loot in the footprints move to the doorstep.
Exploration, wounds, equipment, currency, quests and area caches remain intact.
Freshly visited towns generate the buildings normally. All new accessories
retain their category and bonuses in bags, worn slots, floor/cached loot, and
Royal Dungeon evidence.
