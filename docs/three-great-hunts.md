# The Three Great Hunts

**Huntmaster Selene** stands beside the contract table in **Rosemoor's
Adventurer's Guild**. Approach her and press **T**, then **Y** to accept this
one-time contract. Three dangerous groups have made the Guild's expedition
routes unsafe.

| Hunt | Destination | Assigned enemies | Route |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rimefang's pack | Frostfell Wastes, stage 3 | Rimefang, two additional Ice Wolves, and a Frost Wraith | Rosemoor's north gate |
| Shardwarden's group | Glassdeep Caverns, stage 4 | Shardwarden and two Blind Stalkers | Stillbury's south gate |
| Dunehex's group | Sunscar Wastes, stage 3 | Dunehex and two Mummies | Stillbury's west gate |

Rimefang is an Ice Wolf, Shardwarden a Shard Golem, and Dunehex a Djinn. Each
leader receives a 25% health increase, rounded up, and retains its species'
ordinary abilities. Hunt enemies have gold corner marks, and each leader
displays its name above its health bar. Groups appear near an ordinary floor
in their stage's middle clearing, among the region's existing terrain.

Complete the three hunts in any order. A hunt completes automatically when
its leader and every assigned companion have been defeated, including enemies
lured away from their starting positions. Ordinary enemies elsewhere can
remain alive. No trophy pickup, inventory space, or regional boss victory is
required. Hunt leaders use ordinary gold drops and do not award boss items.

The journal records each region, stage, and completed hunt. Once all three are
finished, **return and speak to Huntmaster Selene in Rosemoor's Adventurer's
Guild** for **200 gold and 1,500 score**, awarded once. Completed objectives
remain in the journal after claiming the reward.

Accepting the quest preserves explored maps, ordinary enemy populations, boss
victories, and portals. Previously visited or cleared hunt stages gain only
their assigned hunt group when revisited. Encounter placement, enemy damage,
defeats, and rewards persist through backtracking, portals, and saving.

Save version **123** records quest state, completed hunts, and placement of
each individual group member. This also preserves partially placed encounters
without reviving a killed leader or duplicating its companions. Older saves
receive an available quest without losing progress. Saved Guild interiors gain
Selene, with overlapping players and dropped items moved safely beside her.
