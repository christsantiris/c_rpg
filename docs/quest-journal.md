# Quest Journal

Press `Q` during play to open the Quest Journal. It tracks the dungeon,
mountain, forest, and coast quests, Nahla's Ruined Temple quest, and Rook's
one-time labyrinth
quest after they are accepted.

Bram offers **Save Elowen** in **Oakhaven's Inn**. Defeating the Lich on floor 5
frees her automatically, including without accepting the quest or with a full
journal. The rescue appears in **Completed** immediately; its return reminder
directs the player to reunite her with Bram for **80 gold once**. Elowen follows
safely until that conversation and then becomes a permanent Inn resident.

After the Lich King's defeat, **Dain and Alder arrive at Oakhaven's Inn**.
Dain's **Recover the Treasure Map** collects two halves on **Mountains 1 and 2**;
Alder's **The Lost Rangers** frees two spider-held rangers on **Forest 1 and 2**.
Return to the Inn after the second objective: Dain pays 60 gold and 400 score,
and Alder pays 70 gold and 500 score, each once. Accepting either first part
preserves explored stages; its encounters appear on revisiting stages 1 and 2.

Each turn-in unlocks a separate boss follow-up: **Defeat the Goblin King** or
**Defeat the Necromancer**. These can be accepted or declined and use the same
three active slots. The completed first part remains in Completed. Boss victory
finishes the follow-up immediately; its entry points onward to Stillbury or
Dawnmere and requires no second Inn turn-in. A boss defeated earlier receives
credit when the first part is turned in. Physical boss access never depends on
NPC conversations or quest acceptance.

Mara, Ilya and Rook have left Oakhaven in V2; their journal entries keep any
existing progress, but no one offers these quests until a later act.

Mara assigns **Relight the Drowned Beacons** in **Oakhaven's Inn**.
The beacons remain on Sunken Coast stages 2, 3, and 4. Return to her in the
Inn for 80 gold and 600 score; her location change preserves existing
quest progress.

Ilya assigns **The Dragon's Hoard** in **Oakhaven's Inn**. Its objective
remains the golden goblet on Dragonspine stage 5, beyond Ridgeshire's north
gate. Return to Ilya in the Inn for one Potion of Strength and 600 score.

Zara's **The Lost Magic Lamp** quest also appears after accepting it inside
Stillbury's Tavern. Recover the lamp from Sunscar Wastes stage 4,
then return to Zara for 80 gold and 600 score. Pickup moves the quest to
Completed with its reward pending; speak to Zara to collect it.

Town Hall quest, **Reclaim the Emberforge**, tracks recovery of its mechanism
on Ashen Hollow stage 2 and repair of its furnace on stage 4. Return to Steward
Hadrin in Ridgeshire for 100 gold and 800 score; both objectives stay recorded
after collecting the reward.

Marshal Veyra's **Watchfires of Ridgeshire**, offered in **Ridgeshire's Town
Hall**, spans three regions: **Goblin Mountains 5**, **Ashen Hollow 3**, and
**Dragonspine 3**. Each objective displays its own region and stage. Restore
the watchfires in any order and return for **180 gold and 1,400 score**.
Completed objectives remain recorded after claiming the reward.

Brenna's **The Silent Expedition** appears after talking to her in Stillbury's
Tavern. It tracks the expedition journal on Frostfell stage 2 and Surveyor Fen's
rescue on stage 4. Recover the journal with `A`, then defeat Fen's captors and
speak to him with `T`. Return to Brenna in Stillbury's Tavern for 100 gold and 800 score. The Polar
Kraken is optional, and both completed objectives remain in the journal after
collecting the reward.

Orin's **Reclaim the Quarry** tracks the West Winch, Workshop Hoist and Main
Gantry on Stonewake stages 2, 3 and 4. Defeat their guards and press `A` beside
each mechanism to repair it. Return to Orin in Rosemoor's Guild for 120 gold,
1,000 score and Magic Stone Warhammer (+30 maximum HP while equipped). The
journal lists the equipment reward. Orin holds it if the pack is full; repaired
objectives stay recorded after completion. The Quarry Colossus is optional.

Huntmaster Selene offers **The Three Great Hunts** in **Rosemoor's Adventurer's
Guild**. The journal tracks **Rimefang's pack in Frostfell 3**, **Shardwarden's
group in Stonewake 4**, and **Dunehex's group in Sunscar 3**, with each region
and stage displayed separately. Defeating the leader and every assigned
companion automatically completes that hunt; the order is unrestricted.
Return and speak to Selene in Rosemoor's Adventurer's Guild for **200 gold and
1,500 score**. All three objectives remain in the Completed tab afterward.

Brother Oswin's **Rest for the Forgotten** appears after talking to him in
Ridgeshire's Town Hall. Its four separate objectives are the Soldiers',
Watchers', and Choir memorial braziers on Catacombs floors 2, 3, and 4, and the
burial ledger on floor 5. Press `A` beside each; the ledger requires the Grave
Marshal's defeat. Already cold memorials receive credit when the quest is
accepted. Return to Oswin for 150 gold and 1,500 score once. All four objectives
remain recorded after completion, and the quest list scrolls as selection moves
past the visible entries.

**Guide Tomas Home** is offered by Tomas inside the Royal Dungeon after the castle
grounds informant has you arrested. It tracks discovering the secret tunnel
and escorting Tomas to central Ridgeshire. Both objectives occupy a single
tunnel level; reaching town together automatically awards 100 gold and 750
score and moves the quest to Completed. This quest requires no return trip.

The **Active** tab contains quests in progress and shows the optional quest
capacity, such as **ACTIVE 2/3**. You can accept up to **three optional quests**
at once. At the limit, NPCs leave their offers available and explain that an
active quest must be finished before accepting another. Press N or Esc to close
an offer; rejected acceptance never resets a region or changes quest progress.

Quests move to **Completed** as soon as their objectives are finished, freeing
an active slot immediately. If an NPC reward still needs to be collected, the
entry shows **COMPLETED - REWARD PENDING** and retains the return instructions.
Collecting that reward keeps the quest in Completed. Use Tab or Left/Right
to cycle through **Active → Completed → Bosses**.

**Guide Tomas Home** and **Recover Your Equipment** remain visible in Active
when needed, but do not consume optional slots. A full journal cannot prevent
escape or recovery. Older saves keep all accepted quests, even above the limit;
finish enough objectives to bring the optional active count below three before
accepting another. Capacity is derived from existing saved quest states, so
loading preserves progress without introducing a new saved counter.

Each entry shows its quest giver, description, target area and stages, objective progress,
status, gold reward, and score reward. The Bosses tab always lists every
regional boss, including Stonewake Quarry's Quarry Colossus, with their
defeated or undefeated status. It also tracks the castle's armory Vampire, Royal
Arcanist, and Lord Veyr. Miniboss victories remain recorded after retreat;
Veyr's defeat ends the campaign with victory.

The Labyrinth entry tracks the Minotaur guarding Rook's ivory rook. Previous
Maze Warden victories count as completed Minotaur encounters.

Use the up and down arrows to select a quest. Use `Tab`, left, or right to
change tabs. Long entry text wraps inside the details panel, and short windows
tighten its spacing. Windows narrower than about 850 pixels hide the quest list
and give the details the full width; **QUEST 1 OF 3** beside the tabs shows the
selection, and the arrows still step through quests. Press `Q` or `Esc` to return to the game. Completing every
objective moves a quest to `Completed`; pending rewards must still be collected
from the original quest giver, except Tomas's escort reward, awarded on arrival.
Boss victories are recorded immediately and
remain complete during later expeditions.

Act 1's quest givers offer at most two optional quests at a time, so a new V2
character cannot reach the limit yet; automated tests cover it. An existing
save above the limit should retain every quest and display its actual count;
Tomas must still reveal the escape hatch at full capacity.
